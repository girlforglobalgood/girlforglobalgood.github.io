Professional summary

Beyond the day jobWhat I'm Up To Now

Learning Mandarin

I am currently learning Mandarin. Immersing myself in a new language alongside work has been challenging, but it has unlocked a different set of mental muscles that I enjoy exercising. More importantly, it is an active reset for my brain, and it lets me access and understand a new world without needing a translator or subtitles!

Training for caves and oceans

In 2026, one of my major personal milestones has been my health. I have been training specifically to build the strength to enrol for the Son Doong Cave expedition and to take on my Advanced Open Water scuba certification. This journey has taught me discipline, and the joy of working towards something patiently but consistently.

Building small but powerful AI tools for learning

I am exploring the role of AI as an active tutor, and how it can bridge small gaps in basic skills. Projects I've taken on include building a simple AI tool that helps my intern practise framing sentences in formal English, to grow their confidence in writing emails; developing and delivering an AI-fluency workshop with external experts for participants of the Naz Young People's Initiative; and using Claude to build my own Mandarin tutor.
